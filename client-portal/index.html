Question guidance

Private does not mean urgent.

Portal messaging is for non-urgent care coordination, appointment questions, and follow-up. It is not an emergency service or a substitute for medical care.

✦

Private care questions

For non-urgent questions about your appointment, follow-up, or care coordination, request verified client access first. The public page does not collect or transmit private care details.
