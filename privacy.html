Privacy Policy

We handle client communications with care. This policy explains what information Ellis Restorative Therapies collects through its public website and how clients can contact the practice about it.

Scope and contact

This Privacy Policy applies to restorewithellis.com, its public booking flow, and communications submitted through the public website. Separate client-portal practices are described in the Client Portal Privacy Policy.

Ellis Restorative Therapies is located at 2209 Coffee Rd, Suite M, Modesto, CA 95355. Questions about this policy may be sent to restorewithellis@gmail.com or raised by phone at (209) 450-5296.

Information we receive

Information is collected only when you choose to provide it or when a service needs it to operate. This can include:

  • contact information such as your name, email address, and phone number;
  • booking details such as the therapist selected, requested date/time, session length, and booking notes you choose to provide;
  • messages or inquiries you send to the practice;
  • technical information generated by normal website use, such as device/browser information and hosting security logs; and
  • information needed to administer a voluntary updates, giveaway, or discount opportunity when you opt in.

How information is used

We use information to respond to inquiries, arrange and administer appointments, provide booking confirmations, communicate practice updates you requested, protect the website from misuse, and improve the client experience. We do not sell personal information. We do not use public website submissions to make automated decisions about treatment or eligibility.

Important communication boundary

Do not use public website forms, booking notes, email, or social media to send diagnoses, detailed health histories, treatment notes, insurance identifiers, payment-card details, or emergency information. Discuss care-related details directly with your therapist through the practice’s approved process. For an emergency, contact 911 or the appropriate emergency resource.

Payments and third parties

When a payment link is presented, the transaction is completed by the linked payment provider. Ellis Restorative Therapies does not place payment-card details into this public website. The payment provider’s own privacy terms apply to its checkout.

Sharing, retention, and choices

Information may be shared only with service providers needed to operate the website, booking calendar, email, secure payment link, or hosting infrastructure; with therapists and authorized practice personnel as needed to respond to an appointment or inquiry; or when required by law. We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, operational records, or legal obligations.

You may ask to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information held through these tools, subject to applicable records and legal obligations. Marketing/update messages may be stopped by using the unsubscribe option when provided or by contacting the practice.

California and policy updates

California residents may contact the practice to request information about their personal information rights under applicable law. We may update this policy as website features or legal obligations change; the effective date above will be revised when we do.
