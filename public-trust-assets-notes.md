## Official-mark handling

The inspected CAMTC visual candidate is an unmodified California Massage Therapy Council mark sourced through the council’s public web presence. It must not be stretched, recolored, or used to represent a governmental endorsement. The AMTA member-logo guidance explicitly allows members to use the logo on websites and marketing materials but requires the complete, unaltered member logo supplied by AMTA. The needed downloadable AMTA member asset is account-gated, so it cannot be substituted with a generic or recreated logo.

## Content approach

The site can add a professional **Before Your Session** section now. It should cover booking the right session length, arriving a few minutes early, wearing what is comfortable, sharing relevant comfort preferences directly with the therapist, hydration and after-session pacing as personal comfort considerations, and contacting the practice to reschedule when ill. It should avoid diagnoses, outcomes guarantees, and a request for health history through the public web form.
