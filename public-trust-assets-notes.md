## Official-mark handling

CAMTC’s published terms grant only a personal use license and say its content may not be further published or distributed without advance written permission. Its trademarks, seals, and logos are included in that protected content. Accordingly, the public website uses factual **text-only** CAMTC certification references rather than a copied logo. The AMTA member-logo guidance explicitly allows members to use the supplied unaltered member logo on websites and marketing materials; the AMTA California Chapter member mark supplied by the owner is therefore used without modification.

## Content approach

The site can add a professional **Before Your Session** section now. It should cover booking the right session length, arriving a few minutes early, wearing what is comfortable, sharing relevant comfort preferences directly with the therapist, hydration and after-session pacing as personal comfort considerations, and contacting the practice to reschedule when ill. It should avoid diagnoses, outcomes guarantees, and a request for health history through the public web form.
