Cancellation & Practice Policy

⏱
24-Hour Cancellation Notice Required Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice may be charged a 50% session fee. We appreciate your respect for our schedule and other clients waiting for appointments.
🚫
No-Show Policy No-shows without notice are charged the full session fee. If something comes up unexpectedly, please reach out as early as possible — we're always willing to work with you.
🤒
Illness — Please Reschedule If you are feeling ill, please reschedule. Cancellation fees are always waived for illness with reasonable notice. Your wellbeing — and that of other clients — comes first.
💳
Payment Due at Time of Service We accept cash and all major credit cards. 60 min $100 · 90 min $150 · 120 min $200. Prices are per session, no hidden fees.
🔒
Privacy & Confidentiality All client information is kept strictly confidential and never shared with third parties. Your health history and intake information are fully protected.
