Google states that complete and accurate Business Profile information, verification, review responses, photos, relevance, distance, and prominence all contribute to local visibility; no provider can guarantee or buy a better local rank.[1] Google also requires structured data to represent visible, accurate page content and does not guarantee rich-result appearance even when markup is valid.[2][3]
