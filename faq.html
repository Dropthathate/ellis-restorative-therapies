Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about booking, the client portal, privacy, and payment questions.

How do I book with Zachary or Hunter?

Open the booking page, choose your therapist first, then view that therapist’s live availability. Zachary’s regular online schedule is Monday–Thursday. Hunter’s is Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–1:00 PM, plus first- and third-weekend mornings, 9:00 AM–1:00 PM.

What session lengths and prices are shown online?

Both therapists offer standard 60-minute ($100), 90-minute ($150), and 120-minute ($200) session options. Availability can vary based on the selected therapist’s calendar and session length.

What happens after I submit an online booking?

The booking workflow checks calendar availability, creates the appointment record, adds the booking to the practice booking sheet, and sends a confirmation to the contact information supplied in the booking flow.

What is the client portal for?

The client portal is a separate secure space for practice updates, discount opportunities, event-code benefits, payment links, and brief general questions. It is not a medical record or emergency communication channel.

Can I send a health history or treatment details through the portal?

No. Please do not put diagnoses, symptoms, detailed health histories, treatment notes, insurance identifiers, payment-card details, or emergency information into public forms, portal questions, booking notes, email, or calendar titles. Discuss care-related information directly with the practice through the appropriate channel.

Does the practice accept HSA/FSA?

Payment eligibility can vary by account, provider, and transaction. Contact the practice before booking if you have an HSA/FSA question so current payment details can be confirmed directly.
